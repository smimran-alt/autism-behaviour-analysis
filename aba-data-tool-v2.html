2. Recording guide

Response codes

I — Independent correct
P — Prompted correct
N — Incorrect or no response
— — Not recorded (excluded)

Independent % = independent responses ÷ recorded trials × 100. Prompted and incorrect percentages are tracked separately.

Three baseline days are a tracking example, not a universal clinical requirement. Interpret results using your own approved protocol.
